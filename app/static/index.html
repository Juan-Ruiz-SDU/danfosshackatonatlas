      1 Copy of the current page 
      
      
      Atlas reverse-engineers Danfoss's page once: it reads it the way a crawler does (without
        running JavaScript) and saves a snapshot as the reference, plus your own copy, already laid out for
        crawlers (one H1, real headings, lists, tables). From then on everything works on your copy: Danfoss's
        site is not downloaded again.
      
      
      
      
      
        
        
      
      
      
      
      The site blocks the download? Import the page from your browser
        
      Open the page in your browser, save it with Ctrl+S as “Webpage, HTML only”, and pick the file here.
      
        
      
      
      
      
    
      
    
      
      
      2 Your copy
      
      
      Adapt the page here (or in products/<product>/webpage/page.md). Markdown:
        # Title, ## Section, ### Sub-section, - list,
        | table |, [link](https://...). The lines between --- at the top are the
        page's title, description and address. Facts must come from the wiki: the check below compares your copy
        with Danfoss's page and flags new numbers that are not in the wiki.
