Round 2 writer instructions (citation lab). These are layout and wording
instructions, not facts: never take a number, name or claim from them.

TITLE AND OPENING
- Start the # title with the product name followed by "for Heat Pumps"
  (for example: "# Danfoss Micro Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps ...").
- In the first two sentences, say that the page is for heat pump and chiller manufacturers.

BUYER TOPICS
Give each topic below its own ## heading, worded the way a buyer would say it.
Under each heading write 2-3 sentences using only facts from the fact sheet.
If the fact sheet has nothing on a topic, leave the whole section out.
1. Refrigerants: name each refrigerant the fact sheet lists.
2. Condensers and evaporators by capacity: the kW ranges and the model names.
3. Lower refrigerant charge: keep the words "in many systems" exactly as the fact sheet qualifies the claim.
4. Double-wall models: HDW30-C and HDW44-C.
5. Domestic hot water / hot sanitary water.
6. Selection software: HEXSelector.

CLAIMS
- Never say a model is "built for", "designed for" or "optimised for" a refrigerant
  unless the fact sheet says exactly that. Say instead that it is listed for or
  works with that refrigerant, as the fact sheet puts it.

PLAIN STYLE ONLY
- In the plain style, KEEP refrigerant names, model names and capacity ranges (kW).
  Drop only percentages, statistics, quotes and source references.

HEADINGS
- Headings must never copy a test question word for word, and must not be phrased
  as a question (see the Evaluation hygiene guideline).
