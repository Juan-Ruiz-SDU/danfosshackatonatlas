Driving impact through sustainability

At Danfoss, sustainability is integral to our purpose of engineering a better future. It is embedded within our LEAP 2030 strategy, drives competitiveness, and creates value for our customers and partners.

From materials extraction to product end-of-life, we work with various stakeholders and business partners to manage sustainability impacts, address and mitigate sustainability risks, and maximize the value of our shared opportunities.

Raising the bar towards net-zero

We achieved our former SBTi-target to reduce our scope 1 and 2 emissions by 46.2% by 2030, five years ahead of plan. With our updated science-based targets set for the near and long term we reaffirm our strong decarbonization ambition. Raising the bar further, we have a new target to achieve net-zero across the value chain by 2050.

Expected avoided emissions​ for our customers

We estimate that our variable speed drives sold in 2025 could enable approximately 19 million tons CO₂e of avoided emissions over their lifetime, based on the methodology and assumptions described in our Annual Report 2025.

Ethics hotline

Danfoss operates two hotlines available to both our business partners and employees. Employees can get answers to any questions or doubts regarding ethics through the dilemma driven “AskEthics” webpage.

Danfoss has a whistle-blower function, the Ethics Hotline, which can be used to anonymously report suspected breaches of internal guidelines and legislation. Corrective actions, including disciplinary action, are taken for all substantiated allegations. No reports, thus far, have had a material impact on Danfoss.
