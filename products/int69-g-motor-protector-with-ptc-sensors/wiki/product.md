# What it is
The INT69 G is a motor protector based on PTC sensors that provides effective and reliable protection against thermal overload of electrical machines.

# Who it is for
The INT69 G is suitable for installation in electrical equipment or in the terminal boxes of electrical machines.

# Main problem it solves
The INT69 G solves the problem of thermal overload in electrical machines by monitoring temperature using PTC sensors and switching off the motor protector when temperatures reach critical levels.

# Key features
- Connects up to nine DIN 44081, DIN 44082 conform PTC sensors
- Sensors with different nominal response temperatures can be used
- Relay switch output is a potential free change-over contact
- Sensor, relay and supply circuits are galvanically isolated from each other
- Protection class acc. to EN 60529 IP00
- Connection type: Flat plug sleeves (PTC), screw terminals
- Housing material: PA glass-fibre-reinforced
- Mounting: Can be snapped onto 35mm standard rail as per EN 60715 or screw mounted
- Dimensions: Refer to dimensions in mm
- Weight: Approx. 200g
- Check base: EN 61000-6-3, EN 61000-6-2
- EN 61010-1
- Overvoltage category II
- Pollution level 2
- Approval: UL File No. E75899 cURus

# Typical uses
The INT69 G is used in electrical machines and equipment where thermal overload protection is required.
