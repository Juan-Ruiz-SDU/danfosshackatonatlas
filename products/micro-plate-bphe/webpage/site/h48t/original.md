# Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchanger for transcritical systems

New Micro Plate™ brazed heat exchanger (H48T-CH) dedicated for heat recovery systems in supermarkets and hypermarkets

#### Compact, high-performance CO₂ heat exchanger designed for demanding transcritical environments

Growing our already extensive brazed plate heat exchanger portfolio, we proudly unveil our latest innovation within the Micro Plate™ technology.

Specially designed for CO2 transcritical systems, this state-of-the-art brazed heat exchanger (model H48T-CH) meet the demanding requirements for heat recovery systems in supermarkets and hypermarkets.

Specially designed for CO2/water transcritical systems, this state-of-the-art brazed heat exchanger (model H48T-CH) meet the demanding requirements for heat recovery systems in supermarkets and hypermarkets. With the unique Micro Plate™ technology, the heat exchanger pushes the boundaries of thermal performance and makes it an unmatched and reliable heat exchanger in a CO2 system. A lightweight and compact design without a reinforced frame ensures easy installation and allows for more compact CO2 systems.

Revolutionary Micro Plate™ technology Danfoss’ Micro Plate™ technology is setting new standards in energy efficiency. Significantly improved heat transfer, reduced use of raw materials and minimal hold-up volumes bring clear economic and environmental savings. In addition, the unique Micro Plate™ pattern makes the heat exchanger more robust. In a direct comparison with a traditional brazed plate heat exchanger (fishbone/chevron plate design) the differences are stunning.

- Specially designed for transcritical systems
- Functions as a gas cooler, condenser, sub-cooler, and economizer
- Micro Plate™ design for superior heat transfer and lower pressure loss
- Ideal for heat recovery systems in supermarkets and hypermarkets

#### Technical Specifications

- Dimension: 126 x 386 mm (4.96” x 15.2”)
- Max. number of plates: 150
- Plate material: AISI 316L
- Cover material: AISI 304L
- Brazing material: Copper
- Connection material: AISI 304L
- Working temperatures from -196°C (-320°F) to 200°C (390°F)
- Max. working pressure, primary side: 140 bar (2030 psi)
- Max. working pressure, secondary side: 90 bar (1305 psi)
- Capacity up to 150 kW
- Connections from 1/4 to 1”

## Applications

#### Heat reclaim technology maximizes energy efficiency

CO₂ is a highly suitable refrigerant for heat reclaim. By closely aligning heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration systems, you save money, safeguard stock, and reduce your environmental impact.

The Danfoss Heat Recovery Unit (HRU) helps to eliminate the technical challenges of managing heat recovery. The HRU is an integrated solution managing and buffering the heat from the refrigeration pack – to be reused for space heating, hot tap water, or even old to neighbors or district heating grids.
