# Optimized R290 brazed plate heat exchangers: Lower charge and higher heat transfer
Monday, September 30, 2024
New and extended Micro Plate™ brazed heat exchanger portfolio for residential R290 heat pumps
Growing our already extensive brazed plate heat exchanger portfolio we proudly unveil our latest innovations featuring the Micro Plate™ technology.
With increasing regulations on the climate harming F-gas refrigerants worldwide, Danfoss steps up to meet the demand for products that operate with more climate-friendly natural refrigerants, such as R290 propane.
Danfoss has introduced three innovative brazed plate heat exchangers designed for residential propane heat pumps. Leading the lineup is the H39L-EZU, which boasts a low channel volume and minimal water-side pressure drop, enhancing efficiency. The H62L-CZ features an asymmetric plate design, specifically tailored for R290 reversible heat pumps. Completing the trio is the HDW30-C, an ultra-safe solution for condenser or desuperheater applications, ensuring that fluids remain securely separated to prevent any cross-contamination.

Danfoss provides heat pump manufacturers (OEMs) with expert support in comfort and industrial applications, encompassing system design, performance optimization, simulation tools, testing facilities, and manufacturing simplification. OEMs can accelerate the green transition with the new and extended brazed plate heat exchanger range for residential and light commercial heat pumps. “We are excited to announce the latest extensions of our propane heat pump portfolio. It’s designed to advance energy efficiency and to lower the carbon footprint of heat pumps. This innovative series reflects our ongoing commitment to providing cutting-edge solutions that reduce carbon emissions while delivering reliable, sustainable and cost optimized heat transfer solutions. As we continue to evolve with the changing energy landscape, propane heat pumps offer a forward-thinking approach to cleaner, more efficient energy use in both residential and commercial applications.”
## Product range
The new propane H39L-EZU evaporator can function efficiently as an evaporator or a condenser, providing you with a state-of-the-art heat transfer solution for your reversible system. In addition to Micro Plate™ technology, the H39L-EZU has a special mixing chamber that creates excellent conditions for two-phase refrigerants and features superheating regions that enable optimal phase shift. This results in great refrigerant distribution with no degradation on part load and means that the heat exchanger is less sensitive to the refrigerant’s connection size.
Connection material: AISI 304L
Working temperatures from -196°C (-320 °F) to 200°C (390 °F)
Max. working pressure: 49 bar (711 psi) ref. side / 25 bar (363 psi) water side

The H62L-CZ is a condenser optimized for use in R290 heat pumps and chillers. The new asymmetric Z-design Micro Plates™ has different flow channels on the refrigerant side which improve heat transfer, reduce the amount of material used and minimize the pressure loss on the water side.
Connection material: AISI 304L
Working temperatures from -196°C (-320 °F) to 200°C (390 °F)
Max. working pressure: 49 bar (711psi) ref. side / 25 bar (363 psi) water side
Download H62L-CZ data sheet
HDW30-C & HDW44-C double wall condenser / desuperheater
HDW30-C and HDW44-C are double wall heat exchangers designed for applications where the liquids are not allowed to mix. A double wall heat exchanger prevents the primary flow to be mixed with the secondary flow if there is an internal leak in the unit by leading the leaking liquid out of the heat exchanger. The main applications are condensers or desuperheaters in heat pump systems for hot sanitary water.
Plate material: AISI 316Ti
Connection material: AISI 304L/AISI 316L (water side)
Working temperatures from -196°C (-320 °F) to 200°C (390 °F)
Max. working pressure: 35 bar (508psi) ref. side / 16 bar (232 psi) water side
