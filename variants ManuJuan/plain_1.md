# Danfoss Micro Plate™ Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps  

Danfoss Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchangers are compact, sealed units designed for use in heat pumps and related systems. They are constructed from thin stainless-steel plates brazed together with copper or nickel, forming separate channels for hot and cold fluids. The brazed design eliminates the need for gaskets, reducing maintenance requirements and ensuring long-term reliability. These heat exchangers are tailored for applications requiring efficient heat transfer, particularly in variable-load systems where part-load performance is critical.  

The Micro Plate™ technology features a patented dimple pattern on the plates, replacing the traditional Fishbone (herringbone) corrugation. This design enhances thermal efficiency by creating turbulence in fluid streams while maintaining low pressure drop. The flat contact points between plates minimize tangent stress, ensuring structural integrity. The dimpled pattern also increases the heat exchange surface area, promoting even refrigerant distribution and reducing stagnant zones. These characteristics contribute to improved part-load efficiency and support the use of lower refrigerant charges in many systems.  

A mixing chamber within the heat exchanger ensures balanced refrigerant distribution between channels, maintaining performance even at part load. This design also makes the unit less sensitive to the direction of refrigerant flow, offering flexibility in installation. The Z-design series further optimizes performance by using an asymmetric Z-shaped flow pattern. This configuration allows for achieving the same heat transfer capacity with fewer plates, reducing material use, weight, and environmental impact. The Z-design is optimized for multiple low-GWP refrigerants, aligning with modern sustainability goals.  

For applications where liquids must not mix, a double wall option is available. This design uses two plates per flow channel, preventing internal leakage and making any leaks visible from the outside. It is suitable for systems such as heat pumps, boilers, and transformer oil cooling.  

The Micro Plate™ heat exchangers are used in heat pumps, chillers, district energy systems, and domestic hot water applications. They are designed for manufacturers of heat pumps and chillers, particularly in medium to large commercial buildings. Danfoss provides a selection tool, HEXSelector, to help choose the appropriate heat exchanger. Testing and development are conducted through Danfoss Application Development Centers, which collaborate with customers on heating and cooling systems using various refrigerants.
