# Danfoss Micro Plate™ Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps  

Danfoss Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchangers are compact, sealed units designed for heat pumps and other applications requiring efficient heat transfer. Constructed from thin stainless-steel plates brazed together with copper or nickel, the exchangers create separate channels for hot and cold fluids. This brazed design eliminates the need for gaskets, reducing maintenance requirements and ensuring long-term reliability.  

The product is tailored for manufacturers of heat pumps and chillers, particularly in medium to large commercial buildings. It addresses the challenge of maintaining efficient heat transfer in systems with variable loads, where part-load performance is critical. The design ensures consistent thermal efficiency even under fluctuating operational conditions.  

Key features of the Micro Plate™ technology include a patented dimple pattern on the plates, replacing the traditional Fishbone corrugation. These dimples generate turbulence in fluid streams, enhancing heat exchange while minimizing pressure drop. Flat contact points between plates reduce tangent stress, improving structural integrity. The dimpled pattern also increases surface area and promotes even refrigerant distribution, reducing stagnant zones.  

A mixing chamber within the exchanger ensures uniform refrigerant distribution between channels, maintaining performance even at part load. This design also makes the unit less sensitive to refrigerant connection orientation. The Z-design series offers an asymmetric flow pattern that keeps liquid and vapor phases mixed, enabling the same heat transfer capacity with fewer plates. This reduces material use, weight, and environmental impact while optimizing compatibility with multiple low-GWP refrigerants.  

For applications where fluids must not mix, a double-wall option is available. This design uses two plates per flow channel, preventing internal leakage and allowing external detection of any issues.  

The Micro Plate™ exchangers are suitable for heat pumps, chillers, district energy systems, and domestic hot water applications. They are part of Danfoss’s broader brazed plate heat exchanger portfolio, which includes alternative plate technologies like Fishbone, suited for steady-load systems.  

Danfoss provides a selection tool, HEXSelector software, to help users choose the appropriate exchanger. Testing and development are conducted through Danfoss Application Development Centers, which collaborate with customers on heat pump, chiller, and VRF systems using various refrigerants.
