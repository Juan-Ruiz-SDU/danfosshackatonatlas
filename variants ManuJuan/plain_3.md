# Danfoss Micro Plate™ Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps  

Danfoss Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchangers are compact, sealed units designed for heat pumps and chillers. They consist of thin stainless-steel plates brazed together with copper or nickel, forming separate channels for hot and cold fluids. This brazed design eliminates the need for gaskets, reducing maintenance requirements while ensuring a durable, leak-proof solution. The heat exchangers are tailored for applications in medium to large commercial buildings, where efficient heat transfer is critical, particularly in systems with variable loads.  

The Micro Plate™ design features a patented dimple pattern, replacing the traditional Fishbone (herringbone) corrugation. These dimples create turbulence in fluid streams, enhancing thermal efficiency while maintaining low pressure drop. The flat contact points between plates minimize tangent stress, ensuring structural integrity. The pattern also increases heat exchange surface area and promotes even refrigerant distribution, reducing stagnant zones. This design supports improved part-load efficiency and may enable a lower refrigerant charge in certain systems.  

A mixing chamber within the heat exchanger improves refrigerant distribution between channels, ensuring consistent performance even at part load. This design also makes the unit less sensitive to the direction of refrigerant flow. The Z-design series offers an asymmetric flow path, allowing for reduced plate count compared to conventional designs. This reduces raw material use, weight, and environmental impact while maintaining heat transfer capacity. The Z-design is optimized for multiple low-GWP refrigerants, including R32, R290, R410A, R454B, R454C, and R455A.  

For applications where liquids must not mix, a double-wall option is available. This design uses two plates per flow channel, preventing internal leakage and making any leaks visible from the outside. It is suitable for systems such as heat pumps, boilers, and desuperheaters.  

Danfoss Micro Plate™ heat exchangers are used in heat pumps, chillers, district energy systems, and domestic hot water applications. Compared to alternative plate technologies like Fishbone, which are suited for steady-load systems, Micro Plate™ is optimized for variable-load environments where part-load performance is essential.  

Danfoss provides a selection tool, HEXSelector software, to help users choose the appropriate brazed plate heat exchanger. The company also collaborates with customers to test heating and cooling systems, including heat pumps and VRF systems, using refrigerants such as HFCs, CO2, and flammable alternatives.
