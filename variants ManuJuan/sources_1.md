# Danfoss Micro Plate™ Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps  

Danfoss A/S offers a complete portfolio of brazed plate heat exchangers (BPHE) for heating, cooling, and refrigeration, including the **Danfoss Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchangers** specifically designed for heat pumps. These compact heat exchangers are engineered to enhance thermal efficiency in variable-load systems, addressing the unique challenges of heat pump applications.  

The **Micro Plate™** design features a patented dimple plate pattern, replacing the traditional Fishbone (herringbone) corrugation. This dimple pattern creates turbulence in fluid streams, improving thermal efficiency while maintaining low pressure drop. Flat contact points between plates minimize tangent stress, and the pattern increases heat exchange surface area, ensuring even refrigerant distribution and reducing stagnant zones. According to the Danfoss eBrochure "Danfoss brazed plate heat exchangers" (AD565353287087en-000101, © Danfoss 06-2026), the dimple design is best suited for variable-load systems where part-load performance is critical.  

Key features of the Micro Plate™ heat exchangers include:  
- **Mixing chamber**: Enhances refrigerant distribution between channels without performance degradation, even at part load. This design also makes the unit less sensitive to refrigerant connection orientation.  
- **Z-design series**: An asymmetric Z-shaped flow pattern that keeps liquid and vapour phases well mixed, enabling the same heat transfer capacity with fewer plates. This reduces raw material use, product weight, and environmental impact.  
- **Double wall option**: Two plates per flow channel prevent liquid mixing, making internal leaks visible from the outside. This is particularly useful in applications such as heat pumps, boilers, and transformer oil cooling.  

The Micro Plate™ heat exchangers are used in **heat pumps, chillers, district energy systems, and domestic hot water systems**. They are part of Danfoss’s 11 heat pump platforms, which cover capacities from 2 kW to 300 kW. These platforms include models like the **HDW30-C** (double wall condenser) and **HDW44-C** (double wall condenser), as detailed in the eBrochure.  

For selection, Danfoss provides **HEXSelector** software, which helps identify the optimal heat exchanger for specific applications. Testing and application support are also available through **Danfoss Application Development Centers (ADCs)**, which evaluate systems including heat pumps, chillers, and VRFs using HFCs, CO₂, and flammable refrigerants.  

The **Fishbone plate pattern**, the traditional corrugation design, is also offered by Danfoss for steady-load applications and cost-focused projects. However, the Micro Plate™ technology is specifically tailored for systems requiring high part-load efficiency.
