# Danfoss Micro Plate Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps  

Danfoss A/S offers a complete portfolio of brazed plate heat exchangers (BPHE) for heating, cooling, and refrigeration, including the **Danfoss Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchangers** specifically designed for heat pumps. These compact BPHEs are engineered to enhance heat transfer efficiency in variable-load systems, addressing the unique challenges of part-load performance.  

The **Micro Plate™ plate pattern** is a patented design featuring a dimple pattern instead of the classic Fishbone (herringbone) corrugation. This innovation creates turbulence in fluid streams, improving thermal efficiency while maintaining low pressure drop. Flat contact points minimize tangent stress at contact points, and the pattern increases heat exchange surface area, ensuring even refrigerant distribution and reducing stagnant zones. According to the Danfoss eBrochure, the Micro Plate™ is best suited for variable-load systems where part-load performance is critical.  

Key features of the Micro Plate™ include a **mixing chamber** that enhances refrigerant distribution between channels without performance degradation at part load. This design also makes the unit less sensitive to refrigerant connection orientation. Additionally, the **Z-design series** employs an asymmetric Z-shaped flow to keep liquid and vapour phases well mixed, enabling the same heat transfer capacity with fewer plates. This reduces raw material use, product weight, and environmental impact, as noted in the eBrochure.  

For applications requiring complete separation of fluids, the **double wall option** uses two plates per flow channel instead of one, preventing liquid mixing and making internal leaks visible from the outside. This feature is particularly useful in systems such as heat pumps, boilers, and transformer oil cooling.  

Danfoss Micro Plate™ heat exchangers are available in **11 heat pump platforms**, covering capacities from 2 kW to 300 kW. These platforms include models like the HDW30-C (double wall condenser) and HDW44-C (double wall condenser), with supported refrigerants such as R32, R290, R410A, and others. The **HEXSelector** software aids in selecting the optimal heat exchanger, while **Application Development Centers (ADCs)** provide testing for heating and cooling systems, including heat pumps and chillers.  

The **Fishbone (herringbone)** plate pattern, the classic alternative, is suited for steady-load applications and cost-focused projects. Danfoss offers both technologies, with Micro Plate™ tailored for variable-load systems.
