# Danfoss Micro Plate Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps  

Danfoss A/S offers a complete portfolio of brazed plate heat exchangers (BPHE) for heating, cooling, and refrigeration, including the **Danfoss Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchangers** specifically designed for heat pumps. These compact BPHEs are engineered to enhance heat transfer efficiency in variable-load systems, addressing the unique demands of modern heat pump applications.  

The **Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchanger** is constructed from thin stainless-steel plates brazed together with copper or nickel, forming separate channels for hot and cold fluids. This brazed design ensures a sealed unit with minimal maintenance requirements. The key innovation lies in the **Micro Plate™ plate pattern**, a patented dimple design that replaces the traditional Fishbone (herringbone) corrugation. This dimple pattern creates turbulence in fluid streams, improving thermal efficiency while maintaining low pressure drop, as described in the Danfoss eBrochure (AD565353287087en-000101, 06-2026).  

The Micro Plate™ technology is particularly suited for **variable-load systems** where part-load performance is critical. It includes features such as a **mixing chamber** to enhance refrigerant distribution between channels and a **Z-design series** that ensures liquid and vapor phases remain well mixed. The **double wall option**—two plates per flow channel—prevents liquid mixing and allows internal leaks to be detected externally, making it ideal for applications like heat pumps, boilers, and transformer oil cooling.  

Danfoss provides **11 heat pump platforms** with capacities ranging from 2 kW to 300 kW, supporting refrigerants such as R32, R290, R410A, R454B, R454C, and R455A. Each platform has specific dimensions, capacities, and models, as detailed in the eBrochure. For example, the **HDW30-C** model is a double-wall condenser for platform 30, while the **H129L-CZD** condenser supports capacities up to 300 kW.  

Selection tools like **HEXSelector software** and **Application Development Centers (ADCs)** assist in choosing the optimal heat exchanger. ADCs test systems with customers, including heat pumps, chillers, and VRFs, using refrigerants such as HFCs, CO₂, and flammable alternatives.  

The **Fishbone plate pattern**, the classic corrugation design, remains an option for steady-load applications focused on cost efficiency. However, the Micro Plate™ technology is emphasized for its adaptability to variable-load conditions.
