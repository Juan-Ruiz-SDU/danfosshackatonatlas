# Danfoss Micro Plate Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps  

Danfoss Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchangers are compact, sealed units designed for heat pumps and other applications requiring efficient heat transfer in variable-load systems. Built from thin stainless-steel plates brazed with copper or nickel, these heat exchangers feature separate channels for hot and cold fluids, eliminating the need for gaskets and reducing maintenance. The brazed design ensures durability and reliability in demanding environments.  

The Micro Plate™ technology employs a patented dimple plate pattern, replacing the traditional Fishbone (herringbone) corrugation. This design creates turbulence in fluid streams, enhancing thermal efficiency while maintaining low pressure drop. Flat contact points minimize tangent stress, and the pattern increases heat exchange surface area, ensuring even refrigerant distribution and reducing stagnant zones. The dimple pattern is particularly suited for variable-load systems where part-load performance is critical.  

Key features include a mixing chamber that improves refrigerant distribution between channels without performance degradation, even at part load. The Z-design series offers an asymmetric flow path that keeps liquid and vapour phases well mixed, enabling the same heat transfer capacity with fewer plates. This reduces raw material use, product weight, and environmental impact. A double wall option uses two plates per flow channel, preventing liquid mixing and making internal leaks visible from the outside. This is ideal for applications where liquids must not mix, such as heat pumps, boilers, and transformer oil cooling.  

Danfoss Micro Plate™ heat exchangers are used in heat pumps, chillers, district energy systems, and domestic hot water systems. They support refrigerants including R32, R290, R410A, R454B, R454C, R455A. The product range includes 11 heat pump platforms, covering capacities from 2 kW to 300 kW. Platforms include models like H17L-CZ, H22(L)-E, H30-CW, HDW30-C (double wall), H48T-CH, and H129L-CZD. Double wall models include HDW30-C and HDW44-C.
