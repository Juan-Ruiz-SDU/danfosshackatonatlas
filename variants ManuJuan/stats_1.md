# Danfoss Micro Plate™ Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps  

Danfoss Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchangers are designed for heat pumps, offering efficient heat transfer in variable-load systems. These compact heat exchangers are built from thin stainless-steel plates brazed together with copper or nickel, forming separate channels for hot and cold fluids. The brazed design ensures a sealed unit with minimal maintenance needs.  

The Micro Plate™ technology features a patented dimple pattern instead of the classic Fishbone corrugation. This design creates turbulence in fluid streams, enhancing thermal efficiency while maintaining low pressure drop. Flat contact points minimize tangent stress, and the pattern increases heat exchange surface area, distributing refrigerant evenly to reduce stagnant zones. The mixing chamber improves refrigerant distribution between channels, ensuring consistent performance even at part load and reducing sensitivity to refrigerant connection orientation.  

The Z-design series employs an asymmetric Z-shaped flow to mix liquid and vapor phases effectively. This allows achieving the same heat transfer capacity with fewer plates, reducing raw material use, weight, and environmental impact. The design is optimized for multiple low-GWP refrigerants. A double wall option uses two plates per flow channel to prevent liquid mixing, making it suitable for applications where contamination must be avoided, such as heat pumps, boilers, and transformer oil cooling.  

Supported refrigerants include R32, R290, R410A, R454B, R454C, and R455A. Danfoss offers 11 heat pump platforms, each with specific dimensions and capacity ranges:  
- **Platform 17**: Outline 95 x 178 mm, capacity 2–15 kW (models H17L-CZ condenser, H17L-CZ SGHX)  
- **Platform 22**: Outline 76 x 312 mm, capacity 2–20 kW (models H22(L)-E evaporator, H22(L)-C condenser)  
- **Platform 30**: Outline 95 x 320 mm, capacity 3–10 kW (condensers H30(L)-C, H30-CW, HDW30-C double wall)  
- **Platform 34**: Outline 118 x 289 mm, capacity 2–20 kW (SGHX models H34L-CL, H34L-CM)  
- **Platform 39**: Outline 117 x 331 mm, capacity 5–15 kW (evaporator H39L-EZU)  
- **Platform 44**: Outline 119 x 376 mm, capacity 2–15 kW (condenser HDW44-C double wall)  
- **Platform 48**: Outline 126 x 386 mm, capacity 10–150 kW (condensers and SGHX H48T-CH, H48T-CL)  
- **Platform 55**: Outline 109 x 525 mm, capacity 5–40 kW (condenser H55(L)-C)  
- **Platform 62**: Outline 118 x 525 mm, capacity 5–50 kW (evaporators H62(L)-E, H62(L)-EZU; condensers H62(L)-C, H62(L)-CX, H62L-CZ)  
- **Platform 118**: Outline 186 x 613 mm, capacity 30–130 kW (evaporators H118(L)-E, H118L-EZ; condensers H118(L)-C, H118L-CZ)  
- **Platform 129-D**: Outline 246 x 525 mm, capacity 50–300 kW (evaporator H129L-EZD, condenser H129L-CZD)  

The overall heat pump capacity range spans 2 kW to 300 kW. Danfoss HEXSelector software aids in selecting the appropriate heat exchanger, while Application Development Centers (ADCs) test systems with customers, including heat pumps, chillers, and VRFs, using HFCs, CO2, and flammable refrigerants.  

These heat exchangers are ideal for heat pumps, chillers, district energy systems, and domestic hot water applications. Their design addresses challenges in variable-load systems, improving part-load efficiency and enabling lower refrigerant charges.
