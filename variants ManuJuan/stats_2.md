# Danfoss Micro Plate™ Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps  

Danfoss Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchangers are compact, sealed units designed for efficient heat transfer in heat pumps and other applications. Built from thin stainless-steel plates brazed with copper or nickel, these exchangers feature separate channels for hot and cold fluids, eliminating the need for gaskets and reducing maintenance requirements. They are tailored for manufacturers of heat pumps and chillers, particularly in medium to large commercial buildings where variable-load systems demand high part-load efficiency.  

**Key Features**  
- **Micro Plate™ Design**: A patented plate pattern with dimples instead of traditional Fishbone corrugation. Dimples create turbulence in fluid streams, enhancing thermal efficiency while maintaining low pressure drop. Flat contact points minimize tangent stress, increasing heat exchange surface area and ensuring even refrigerant distribution.  
- **Mixing Chamber**: Improves refrigerant distribution between channels without performance loss at part load. The design makes the unit less sensitive to refrigerant connection orientation.  
- **Z-Design Series**: Asymmetric Z-shaped flow keeps liquid and vapor phases mixed, achieving the same heat transfer capacity with fewer plates. This reduces raw material use, weight, and environmental impact. Optimized for multiple low-GWP refrigerants.  
- **Double Wall Option**: Two plates per flow channel prevent liquid mixing, making internal leaks visible externally. Ideal for applications where liquids must not mix, such as heat pumps, boilers, and transformer oil cooling.  
- **Compatibility**: Supports refrigerants including R32, R290, R410A, R454B, R454C, and R455A.

**Applications and Testing**  
Typical uses include heat pumps, chillers, district energy systems, and domestic hot water. Danfoss HEXSelector software aids in selecting the appropriate exchanger. Application Development Centers (ADCs) test systems with heat pumps, chillers, and VRFs using HFCs, CO2, and flammable refrigerants.
