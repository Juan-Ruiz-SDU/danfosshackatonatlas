# Danfoss Micro Plate™ Brazed Plate Heat Exchangers for Heat Pumps  

Danfoss Micro Plate™ brazed plate heat exchangers are compact, sealed units designed for heat pumps, featuring a patented dimple plate pattern to enhance heat transfer efficiency in variable-load systems. These heat exchangers are built from thin stainless-steel plates brazed together with copper or nickel, forming separate channels for hot and cold fluids. The brazed design eliminates gaskets, reducing maintenance needs and ensuring a sealed unit.  

The Micro Plate™ plate pattern replaces the classic Fishbone (herringbone) corrugation with a dimple design. This creates turbulence in fluid streams, improving thermal efficiency while maintaining low pressure drop. Flat contact points minimize tangent stress, and the pattern increases heat exchange surface area, distributing refrigerant evenly to reduce stagnant zones. The design is best suited for variable-load systems where part-load performance is critical, such as heat pumps, chillers, district energy, and domestic hot water systems.  

Key features include a mixing chamber that improves refrigerant distribution between channels without performance degradation, even at part load. The Z-design series employs an asymmetric Z-shaped flow to keep liquid and vapour phases well mixed, enabling the same heat transfer capacity with fewer plates. This reduces raw material use, product weight, and environmental impact. A double wall option uses two plates per flow channel, preventing liquid mixing and making internal leaks visible from the outside. This is particularly useful in applications like heat pumps, boilers, and transformer oil cooling.  

Danfoss offers 11 heat pump platforms, covering capacities from 2 kW to 300 kW. Platforms include models such as H17L-CZ, H22(L)-E, H30-CW, H48T-CH, and H129L-EZD, with supported refrigerants like R32, R290, R410A, R454B, R454C, R455A. Double wall models include HDW30-C and HDW44-C.  

Selection tools like HEXSelector software and Application Development Centers (ADCs) assist in choosing the right heat exchanger. ADCs test systems with customers, including heat pumps, chillers, and VRFs, using HFCs, CO2, and flammable refrigerants.
