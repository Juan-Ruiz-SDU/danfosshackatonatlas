# Policies
* [Single source of truth](source-of-truth.md) - Every fact in generated pages must come from this bundle; every fact in this bundle comes from an official Danfoss document or danfoss.com page, cited with its code (or URL) and date.
* [Never claim](never-claim.md) - Claims that generated pages must never make, because the source gives no data to back them up.
* [Quotes](quotes.md) - Quotes from real people with real permission; none are available yet.
