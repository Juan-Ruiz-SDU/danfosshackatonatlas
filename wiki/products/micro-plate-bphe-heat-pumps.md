# What it is
A compact brazed plate heat exchanger built from thin stainless-steel plates brazed together with copper or nickel. The plates form separate channels for the hot and cold fluids. The brazed design is a sealed unit with no gaskets and limited maintenance needs.[^ebrochure]

# Who it is for
Manufacturers of heat pumps (and chillers), including chiller and heat pump applications in medium to large commercial buildings.

# Main problem it solves
Efficient heat transfer in heat pumps, especially in variable-load systems where part-load performance is important.

# Price
Not published in the brochure.

# Key features
- [Micro Plate™ plate pattern](/technologies/micro-plate-pattern.md): a patented plate design with a dimple pattern instead of the classic Fishbone (herringbone) corrugation.
- [Mixing chamber](/technologies/mixing-chamber.md): improves refrigerant distribution between channels.
- [Z-design series](/technologies/z-design-series.md): an asymmetric Z-shaped flow that keeps liquid and vapour phases well mixed.
- [Double wall option](/technologies/double-wall-option.md): two plates per flow channel instead of one, so the liquids can never mix.

# Typical uses
Heat pumps, chillers, district energy and domestic hot water systems.

# How it differs from alternatives
Only verifiable points: Danfoss offers two plate technologies. [Fishbone](/technologies/fishbone-pattern.md) is the classic, widely used pattern, suited to steady loads and cost-focused projects. [Micro Plate™](/technologies/micro-plate-pattern.md) is suited to variable-load systems where part-load performance matters.
