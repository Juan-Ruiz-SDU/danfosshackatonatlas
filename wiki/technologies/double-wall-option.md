# Facts
- Two plates per flow channel instead of one.[^ebrochure]
- The liquids can never mix, and any internal leak becomes visible from the outside.
- HDW30-C and HDW44-C are double wall heat exchangers designed for condenser or desuperheater applications where the liquids are not allowed to mix.[^r290-ebrochure-p9]
- If there is an internal leak, the double wall design leads the leaking liquid out of the heat exchanger, so the primary flow does not mix with the secondary flow.[^r290-ebrochure-p9]
- Never mixing and visible internal leaks are both very important in applications where safety is a priority.[^r290-ebrochure-p10]
- Double wall units keep the advantages of a regular brazed plate heat exchanger, including compactness.[^r290-ebrochure-p11]

# Used where liquids must not mix
- heat pumps
- boilers
- transformer oil cooling
- desuperheaters
- heat recovery from air compressors

# Double wall models
- HDW30-C: condenser, platform 30 (see [Heat pump platforms](/platforms/heat-pump-platforms.md))
- HDW44-C: condenser, platform 44
- The main applications of the double wall models HDW30-C and HDW44-C are condensers or desuperheaters in heat pump systems for hot sanitary water.[^r290-ebrochure-p9]
