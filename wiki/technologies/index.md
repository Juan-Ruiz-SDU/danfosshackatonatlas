# Plate technologies
* [Micro Plate™ plate pattern](micro-plate-pattern.md) - Patented plate design with a dimple pattern instead of the classic Fishbone (herringbone) corrugation.
* [Fishbone plate pattern](fishbone-pattern.md) - The classic, widely used herringbone plate pattern, suited to steady loads and cost-focused projects.

# Features, series and options
* [Mixing chamber](mixing-chamber.md) - Improves refrigerant distribution between channels, with no performance degradation even at part load.
* [Z-design series](z-design-series.md) - Series with an asymmetric Z-shaped flow that keeps liquid and vapour phases well mixed.
* [Double wall option](double-wall-option.md) - Two plates per flow channel instead of one, so the liquids can never mix and any internal leak becomes visible from the outside.
