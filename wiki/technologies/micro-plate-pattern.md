# How it works
- A patented plate design with a dimple pattern instead of the classic [Fishbone](/technologies/fishbone-pattern.md) (herringbone) corrugation.[^ebrochure]
- The dimples create turbulence in the fluid streams, which improves thermal efficiency while keeping pressure drop low.
- Flat contact points minimise tangent stress at the contact points.
- The pattern increases the heat exchange surface area and distributes refrigerant evenly, minimising stagnant zones.

# Benefits
- Can improve part-load efficiency and help reduce pressure drop.
- In many systems it also enables a lower refrigerant charge.
- The brochure gives no figures for these benefits (see [Never claim](/policies/never-claim.md)).

# Best suited to
Variable-load systems where part-load performance matters.

# Typical uses
Heat pumps, chillers, district energy and domestic hot water systems.
