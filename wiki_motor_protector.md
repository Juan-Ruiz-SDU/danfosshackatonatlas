## Product
- Product name: INT69 G® motor protector
- Order number: 097B51153 (legacy BOCK reference numbers are the same without "097B")
- What it is: an electronic motor protector that works with PTC temperature sensors to protect electrical machines (motors) against thermal overload. The protection device is made up of the INT69 G motor protector plus the PTC sensors.
- Who it is for: builders and installers of electrical machines and cooling installations who need thermal overload protection for motors. Installation must be done by an electrician.
- Main problem it solves: it switches the motor off when a monitored part gets too hot, protecting the motor from thermal overload damage.
- Price: not stated in the data sheet.
- How it works:
  - Up to nine PTC sensors (conforming to DIN 44081 / DIN 44082) can be connected in series to the measuring circuit input.
  - Sensors with different nominal response temperatures can be mixed, so several measuring points can be monitored with one INT69 G.
  - When the temperature at a monitored point reaches the sensor's nominal response temperature, the sensor becomes highly resistive and the motor protector switches off.
  - Lock-out: as delivered, a lock-out bridge is fitted at terminals B1-B2. After the motor cools down, it can only restart once the lock is released by interrupting the supply voltage for more than 5 seconds.
  - Unlocked option: removing bridge B1-B2 turns it into an unlocked version that switches back on automatically after cooldown.
  - The relay output is a potential-free change-over contact working on the closed-circuit principle: the relay also drops to the reset position and shuts off if a sensor or cable fails.
  - Sensor, relay and supply circuits are galvanically isolated from each other.
- Key features:
  - Compact construction, suitable for installation in electrical equipment or in the terminal box of an electrical machine.
  - Fail-safe: shuts off on sensor or cable failure (closed-circuit principle).
  - Can be snapped onto a 35 mm standard rail (EN 60715) or screw-mounted.
  - UL approved (cURus).
- How it differs from alternatives (only verifiable points): the data sheet describes PTC-based motor protection as one of the most effective and reliable protective measures against thermal overload of electrical machines.
- Safety notes from the data sheet: mounting, maintenance and operation must be carried out by an electrician; valid European and national standards for electrical equipment and cooling installations must be observed; sensors and connection lines that extend from the terminal box need at least basic insulation; the supplied terminal cover must be fitted before commissioning.
